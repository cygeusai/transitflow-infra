The closed loop: a tenant files a MAINTENANCE_REQUEST → owner or staff approves → a trigger creates a JOB + WORK_ORDER and links work_order_id back → completion and cost post to LEASE_LEDGER → income/expense roll up into OWNER_STATEMENTS. RENT_PAYMENTS settle via Stripe and post a payment row to the ledger.

Isolation: external users attach to PROPERTY_OWNERS.user_id or LEASE_TENANTS.user_id; every table is company-scoped with Row-Level Security.
